About Tilde

A small store for things you use every day.

Who we are

Tilde started with a simple idea: most people do not need more things, they need better versions of the things they already use. We pick a few products in each category and test them before they reach the shop.

Tilde is a demo store built as a web development internship project at Sysslan IT Solutions.

Mission

Make everyday essentials that are honest in price, simple in design and built to last.

Vision

A home, wardrobe and desk with fewer, better things in them.

What we value

Quality first

Every product is checked for materials, fit and finish.

Fair pricing

Clear prices, visible discounts, no surprise fees at the cart.

Easy returns

Change your mind within 7 days and send it back.
